I work on agentic post-training for long-horizon knowledge work: training models to carry a real task — research, analysis, writing — from the first step to a finished result, over hours of tool use instead of a single reply.

Most recently I worked on Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, a voice model that keeps the conversation going while it reasons and calls tools in the background. Before that: audio-native LLMs, and open models for Traditional Chinese.
